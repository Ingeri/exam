# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to providing a secure, fair, and reliable examination environment for students, instructors, and administrators. All participants using this platform agree to uphold high standards of academic honesty and technical integrity.

## Expected Behavior
* **Academic Honesty:** Students must complete assessments independently unless explicitly permitted otherwise by the instructor.
* **Respectful Communication:** All users must interact respectfully, avoiding malicious behavior, harassment, or disruption of platform services.
* **Authorized Access:** Users must only access exams, questions, and score reports authorized for their specific user account or role (`STUDENT`, `INSTRUCTOR`, `ADMIN`).

## Unacceptable Behavior
* Sharing or publishing active exam keys, questions, or answers during a live examination window.
* Attempting unauthorized data scraping, brute-force key guessing, or bypassing API rate limits.
* Injecting malicious payloads or attempting denial-of-service attacks against the hosting infrastructure.

## Enforcement
Violations of academic integrity or platform security rules may result in immediate exam disqualification, invalidation of scores, and revocation of system access.
